Charles's Jambalaya

Charles's Jambalaya is a specialty dish for occasions any time of the year. Whether it be the middle of Summer or the night before Christmas this is a dish worth salivating over.Fill up your stomach with hearty and healthy ingredients that will recharge your mind and body and rejuvenate your soul. It is a relatively economical dish that does not dig too deeply into your wallet and is also a dish that allows flexibility with ingredients.

Ingredients needed

  • Cooking Oil
  • Andouille Sausage
  • Rotisserie Chicken
  • Minced Garlic
  • Tomato Paste
  • Diced Tomatos
  • Chicken Broth
  • Cajun Seasoning
  • Bay Leaves
  • Rice
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Louisiana Hot Sauce
  • One Onion
  • One Green Pepper
  • One Stalk of Celery

Steps to make Charles's Jambalaya

  1. Chop your sausage
  2. Chop your onion, green pepper, and celery to make your "trinity"
  3. Put a cup of oil into a large pot and dump your sauasge in and sear them
  4. Remove the sausage with a slotted spoon
  5. In the same pot, sautee your trinity
  6. Scrape up all of that "goodness" from the sausage and mix it into your veggies
  7. Add spoon of minced garlic and 2 tbsp of tomato paste
  8. Stir for a couple of minutes
  9. Add diced tomatos
  10. Add three and a half cups of chicken broth
  11. Add cajun seasoning and two bay leaves
  12. Give it a good stir and add two cups of uncooked rice
  13. Add a splash of worcestershire and Louisiana hot sauce for taste
  14. Bring to a boil
  15. Cover and simmer for 20-25 minutes
  16. Mix in your sausage and shredded chicken and let sit for five minutes
  17. Enjoy!
