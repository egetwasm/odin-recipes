Dakota's Pastalaya

Dakota's Louisiana Pastalaya is a rich, savory one-pot dish packed with browned meats, peppers, onions, garlic, and noodles simmered together in a flavorful Cajun-style broth. This hearty meal brings together a perfect mix of smoky sausage, tender pork, and juicy chicken for a satisfying family-style dinner.

Ingredients Needed

  • 1 lb pork
  • 1 lb chicken
  • 1 lb andouille sausage
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 bunch green onions
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • Tony's seasoning
  • Twisty noodles
  • Kosher salt or sea salt
  • Browning sauce
  • Black pepper
  • Minced garlic
  • Louisiana hot sauce or Tabasco
  • Hawaiian rolls or Ritz crackers
  • Oil

Steps to Make Louisiana Pastalaya

  1. Heat the stove to medium-high heat and add enough oil to coat the bottom of the pot.
  2. Season the pork with kosher or sea salt and black pepper, then brown it in the pot for 15-20 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken in cubes and season with Tony's and black pepper. Cook until browned.
  4. Remove the pork and chicken and set them aside.
  5. Add the andouille sausage to the pot and brown it in the remaining oil and meat drippings.
  6. Remove the sausage and set it aside with the pork and chicken.
  7. Add the chopped bell pepper, red pepper, green onion, and garlic to the pot. Cook until softened and slightly browned, stirring often.
  8. Add Tony's seasoning while the vegetables cook and let them develop a little color.
  9. Add Louisiana hot sauce or Tabasco to taste.
  10. Pour in 3 1/2 to 4 cups of water and stir in 1 tablespoon of browning sauce.
  11. Add the cream of chicken soup and stir until combined.
  12. Return the pork, chicken, and sausage to the pot and bring the mixture to a boil.
  13. Once boiling, add the twisty noodles and cook for 5-7 minutes, or until tender.
  14. Transfer the pot to the oven at 300 degrees Fahrenheit and bake for 30 minutes to 1 hour to let the flavors combine and the noodles absorb the broth.
  15. Serve warm with Hawaiian rolls or Ritz crackers.
