Guk's Red Beans and Rice

Guk's red beans and rice is a southern classic that combines soul and love in every bite. This dish is a staple in Louisiana cuisine, known for its rich flavors and hearty ingredients. If you ever find that you want to a meal that reminds you of home, this is the perfect dish to make. The combination of red beans, rice, and spices creates a comforting and satisfying meal that is sure to please everyone at the table.

Ingredients Needed

  • 1 pound Blue runner dried beans
  • 2 32 oz low sodium chicken broth
  • 1 med onion, diced
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 cloves chopped garlic
  • 1 pound to 1.5 pounds Conecuh sausage(sliced bite sizes)
  • 1 smoked pork shank
  • 1 or 2 tbsp. creole seasoning
  • Rice(zataran's-parboiled)

Steps to make Guk's Red Beans and Rice

  1. Boil the sausage and pork shank for about 30 minutes in the four cups of chicken broth
  2. Remove meat, let the liquid cool so the fat rises to the surface(you can put it in the fridge overnight) and can be removed
  3. Add the other 32 oz chicken broth and all the ingredients to the crock pot and cook and cook on high for eight hours(can go for six)
  4. Let sit for two more hours
  5. Done!
